In the COMPAS Recidivism (ProPublica) dataset, the overall favorable outcome (approval) rate is 45.5%. Mutual Information proxy analysis revealed that feature 'priors_count' exhibits the highest demographic leakage with respect to protected attribute 'race' (MI = 0.0329, categorized as 'Moderate Proxy Risk'). This disparity is statistically significant (CI excludes zero), confirming systematic demographic bias. Applying 'Reweighing' mitigation reduced the Demographic Parity disparity from 0.555 to 0.554 (a 0.2% reduction, 95% CI: [0.185, 1.000]). Paired hypothesis testing confirmed this improvement is not statistically significant (p = 0.9103). The associated accuracy/F1 trade-off cost was 0.0 percentage points.
